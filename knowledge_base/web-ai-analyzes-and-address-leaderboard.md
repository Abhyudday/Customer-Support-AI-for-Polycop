### 🥇 1. The Core Score (0 - 100)

The Core Score is the ultimate TL;DR of a wallet's "copyability." It heavily penalizes wallets that are dangerous to follow and rewards high-certainty traders.

* **80 - 100:** Elite. Highly copyable. (Usually high win rate, low slippage, and active right now).
* **60 - 79:** Good. Profitable, but might require you to filter out small trades or endure longer holding periods.
* **0 - 59:** Warning. Even if they have massive historical profits, they might be currently inactive, suffering from extreme slippage, or on a losing streak.

> **⚠️ Why did a whale with +$400,000 PnL get 34 points?** The V2 Engine punishes **"Zombie Whales."** If a whale made all their money months ago but hasn't opened any *new* positions in the last 48 hours, the system lowers their score. Why? Because if you copy them *today*, your funds will just sit idle.

### 📊 2. Decoding Core Metrics

#### Actual PnL vs. Copy Backtest PnL

* **Actual PnL:** The raw profit the whale made.
* **Copy Backtest PnL:** The estimated profit **YOU** would make. It assumes a 2% entry/exit slippage and strictly ignores "old bags" (profits from positions the whale bought months before the tracking window). **Always look at the Copy Backtest PnL.**

#### Copy Loss Rate (The "Slippage" Tax)

This shows how much of the gross profit is eaten by copy-trading friction.

* **< 15%:** Excellent. Usually macro/political traders who hold for days.
* **15% - 25%:** Acceptable, but you are paying a slight premium.
* **> 30%:** **DANGER.** This is a high-frequency scalper. They might make 1% per trade, but your 2% slippage means they win while you bleed out.

#### Last 2D Profit Share (The "Momentum" Indicator)

This metric is crucial. It calculates what percentage of their total profit was generated by **NEW capital deployed in the last 48 hours**.

* If this is **0%** or **< 10%**, the trader is "cooling off" or only settling old bets. Copying them now is risky.
* If this is **> 20%**, they have a "hot hand" and are actively finding winning markets right now.

#### Win Rate & P/L Ratio

* **V2 Rule:** A trade is ONLY counted as a "Win" if the **Copy Backtest PnL >= 0**. If the whale made $0.50 but you lost $1.50 due to slippage, it counts as a loss.
* **Sniper Bonus:** Wallets with a Win Rate > 80% receive a massive score boost. We value users who trade less but win almost every time, as they are safest to copy.

### 🕒 3. The "Recent 20 Markets" Table

This table is your **Zero-Inventory Truth Serum**.

Instead of mixing old and new trades, PolyCop finds the exact moment the whale opened their 20th most recent new market. We pretend your copy-bot was turned on at that *exact second* with an empty inventory.

* **Net PnL:** What the whale made.
* **Backtest Copy PnL:** What your bot would have made.
* **The ✅ / ❌ Icons:** A green checkmark ONLY appears if the *Backtest Copy PnL* is zero or positive. **Do not copy traders whose recent table is a sea of ❌**, even if their historical "Actual PnL" is high.

### 🎯 4. Trader Archetypes: To Copy or Not to Copy?

Before hitting "Start Copy Trade," identify which archetype the wallet belongs to:

#### 🟢 The Sniper (Strong Buy)

* **Metrics:** Win Rate > 80%, Trading Volume is moderate, Copy Loss Rate < 15%.
* **Action:** **COPY.** These traders only shoot when they are sure. You can comfortably allocate a fixed amount per trade to them.

#### 🟡 The Macro Whale (Copy with Patience)

* **Metrics:** High PnL (>$50k), Median Holding Time > 5 Days, Low Win Rate (\~50%) but High P/L Ratio (> 3.0).
* **Action:** **COPY, BUT BE PATIENT.** They lose small bets but win massive ones (e.g., US Elections, Geo-politics). You must be willing to hold your funds in the market for weeks to see the payout.

#### 🔴 The Zombie Whale (WAIT)

* **Metrics:** Massive historical PnL, but Score < 50, Last 2D Profit Share = 0%, Last Active > 3 days ago.
* **Action:** **DO NOT COPY YET.** Keep them on your watchlist. They made a fortune months ago and are currently inactive. Wait until PolyCop shows their "Last Active" as < 1 Day before deploying capital.

***

#### ✅ The 10-Second Checklist Before Copying

1. Is the **Core Score** above 60? (Preferably 80+)
2. Is the **Copy Loss Rate** below 25%?
3. Is the **Last 2D Profit Share** > 10%? (Are they hot *right now*?)
4. In the **Recent 20 Markets**, are there mostly ✅ icons?

If you answered YES to all four, tap **Copy Trade** and let the bot do the rest.

#### Summary: How to Spot the Perfect Copy Target?

When you review a PolyCop AI report, a highly profitable and safe target to copy usually checks these boxes:

1\. PolyCop Score > 60/100.

2\. Backtest Copy Pnl is solidly positive and relatively close to the Actual PnL.

3\. Slippage Cost Rate is low (preferably `< 20%`).

4\. Hedged Markets ratio is minimal (`< 30%`), proving they take directional bets rather than just market-making.

5\. Recent 20 Markets show sustained profitability without a sudden cliff-drop in performance.<br>

You should avoid such examples:

`Actual PnL: +$12,000`

`Backtest Copy Pnl: -$3,000`

`Hedged Markets: 85%`
